About This Project

A VR recreation of Varrock, the iconic city from Old School RuneScape. Compared to the Lord of the Rings projects, this one presented a very different challenge - faithfully recreating a stylised, low-poly aesthetic at full VR scale while maintaining the character and atmosphere of the original. All geometry was modelled in Blender and assembled in Unity, with a focus on accurate layout, scale, and lighting.
