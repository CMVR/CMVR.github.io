Hall of Kings

Lord of the Rings VR Environment

Lord of the Rings · VR Environment · Unity

A VR recreation of the Hall of Kings, capturing the grandeur and atmosphere of Tolkien's world. This project focuses on architectural detail and creating an immersive sense of scale.

Gallery

About This Project

A VR recreation of the Hall of Kings in Minas Tirith, from Peter Jackson's The Return of the King. All geometry was modelled in Blender and assembled in Unity. This was an earlier project - some textures were created from scratch, others were sourced and modified to fit the environment.
