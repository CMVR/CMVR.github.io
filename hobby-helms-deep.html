Helm's Deep

Lord of the Rings VR Environment

Lord of the Rings · VR Environment · Unity

A personal project recreating the fortress of Helm's Deep from The Lord of the Rings in virtual reality. This environment allows users to explore and experience the scale and atmosphere of one of cinema's most iconic battle locations.

Gallery

About This Project

A VR recreation of Helm's Deep from Peter Jackson's The Two Towers. The goal was accuracy - replicating the fortress as it appears in the films with as much fidelity as possible within the constraints of a real-time VR environment.

All assets were built from scratch. Geometry was modelled in Blender, textures created procedurally in Substance Designer and hand-painted in Substance Painter, then assembled and made interactive in Unity.
